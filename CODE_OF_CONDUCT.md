# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and inclusive collaboration environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on technical outcomes.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Support maintainability, reliability, and user safety.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or hostile language
- Deliberate disruption of project workflows

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate actions for violations, including warning, restricted participation, or removal from project spaces.
